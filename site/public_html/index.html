Get Recomp Store

Installs once, then keeps itself and your games up to date.

  1. Jailbreak your PS5 and start your payload loader. Any loader on port 9021 works, such as etaHEN's or elfldr. You'll also need kstuff and ShadowMountPlus 1.7 Beta 5 or newer.
  2. Download the installer payload.
  3. Send it to your PS5. Use any payload sender, or from a computer on the same network: nc -N <your PS5's IP> 9021 < RecompStore-Installer.elf
  4. Open Recomp Store from your home screen. The installer has ShadowMountPlus add it. From then on the store updates itself.
Install by hand instead

Download the Recomp Store zip from its GitHub releases, unzip it, and copy the folder into /data/homebrew/ on your PS5 over FTP. Then register it the way you register your other homebrew.
