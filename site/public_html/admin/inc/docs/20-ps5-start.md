RecompStore installs and updates PS5 homebrew straight from your GitHub releases. Here's how to get your app in and look after it.

## Get a title ID

Every PS5 app needs a title ID no other app uses. Get a free one in the **Title IDs** tab of your hub, or reserve one you picked. It shows as reserved on the Title ID lookup, and nobody else gets it. One that no app comes out with in 6 months goes back, unless you keep it.

## Send your app in

Use **Submit an app** with your GitHub repo, the release file to install, your title ID, and your icon, backdrop and screenshots. We look at every one, test it, and add it to the store.

## Betas

Put out a beta for testers before everyone gets it. You choose who can try it and how many, and add notes for them. Feedback and logs from testers come back to you.

## Logs from players

When something goes wrong, players can send their logs from the store. They show up in the **Logs** tab, sorted by what happened, so you can see which problems come up most.
